## Description (4000 char max)

Photo Point is the fastest way to find, save, and organize locations for your next shoot — built for photographers and videographers who scout on the move.

DISCOVER SPOTS ANYWHERE, EVEN SOMEWHERE NEW
This is what makes Photo Point different: ask any AI chat tool a question like "find abandoned buildings, old gas stations, and roadside oddities near me," paste the response into Photo Point, and every spot gets imported — pinned, geocoded, and ready to scout. Landing in an unfamiliar city on a trip with a few hours to kill? This is how you find the spots locals know about without knowing a single local.

FIND IT, TAP IT, DONE
Driving and spot something worth coming back to? Save it in one tap — in the app, from the Lock Screen widget, or hands-free with "Hey Siri, save this spot." Photo Point captures your GPS coordinates, heading, and the time, so you always know exactly where you were standing and which way you were facing.

SEND IT FROM ANY APP
Spotted a location in a Street View screenshot, a friend's photo, or your own camera roll? Share it straight to Photo Point from any app's share sheet — attach it to a spot you've already saved, or tap the map to drop a pin and create a new one.

KNOW WHEN THE LIGHT WILL BE RIGHT
Every saved spot shows its best golden-hour window based on your heading and the sun's position, plus one-tap links to a shadow simulator and a cloud-cover forecast — so you know exactly when to come back for the shot.

BUILD OUT EVERY LOCATION
- Attach photos and reference images
- Add notes, parking info (with a button to save exactly where you parked), and a shot list checklist
- Tag spots and group them into trips — then move one spot or a whole selection to a different trip in a couple of taps
- See everything on a map, filtered by tag or trip, with a one-tap button to center on where you are right now

GET THERE AND BACK
Open any saved spot directly in Waze or Google Maps, plan a multi-stop route across several spots at once, or drop a pin into Google Street View before you even leave the house.

WORKS EVERYWHERE YOU DO
Photo Point syncs automatically across iPhone, iPad, and Mac via iCloud — no account to create, no server run by us collecting your data. Your spots are private to you.

EXPORT ANYWHERE
Export any spot or trip as KML to open in Google My Maps, or share a batch of locations with another Photo Point user as a simple file.

Built for photographers and videographers who scout locations for a living or for fun — from cinematic landscapes to real estate to family portraits, Photo Point keeps your spots organized so you can focus on the shot.

## App Review Notes
No account or sign-in is required — Photo Point stores data locally and syncs privately via the reviewer's own iCloud account. Location (GPS/heading) access is used to tag saved spots; camera/photo library access is used to attach reference photos. This app does not support WeatherKit — the WeatherKit entitlement, framework linkage, and all related code have been removed as of build 8 and remain removed in this version. New in 1.1: a Share Extension ("Photo Point" in the system share sheet) that imports a shared image into an existing or new spot; it requests its own When-In-Use location permission to pre-place the pin for new spots.
