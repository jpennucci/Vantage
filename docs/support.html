Photo Point Support

Photo Point is a location-scouting app for photographers and videographers, made by an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests can be sent to james.pennucci@gmail.com. I try to respond within a couple of days.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? In your own private iCloud account, synced via CloudKit across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Nothing is sent to a server run by the developer.

Weather isn't showing up on a saved spot. This depends on Apple's WeatherKit service being available for your account; it doesn't affect any other part of the app.

How do I import locations from an AI chat tool? From the main list, tap the Import menu, then "Import via AI Chat" for a ready-made prompt to copy into ChatGPT, Claude, or any other AI tool — paste its reply back in to import.
