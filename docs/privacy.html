Vantage Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Vantage is a location-scouting app for photographers and videographers, made by an independent developer. This page explains what data Vantage handles and how.

What Vantage collects

When you save a spot, Vantage records:

  • GPS coordinates and compass heading
  • Date and time
  • Any title, notes, tags, parking notes, or shot-list items you type in
  • Photos you attach (captured or chosen from your photo library)
  • A weather summary for that location and time, when available

Vantage also briefly checks your device's motion state (e.g. walking vs. driving) at the moment you save a spot, to suggest an automatic tag. This happens on your device and is not stored as raw motion data — only the resulting tag (like "Driving" or "On Foot") is saved.

Where your data goes

Your data stays in your own iCloud account. Vantage stores everything in Apple's CloudKit, using your personal private iCloud database, so it can sync between your own devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac). The developer of Vantage does not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to your entries, photos, or location history.

Vantage does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or trackers, and does not sell or share your data with third parties.

Apple services used

Vantage uses the following Apple frameworks, subject to Apple's own privacy terms:

  • CloudKit — to sync your entries across your own devices
  • WeatherKit — to look up weather conditions for a saved location
  • Core Location — to record where you are when you save a spot
  • Geocoding (CLGeocoder) — to convert a typed address into coordinates, when you use that import option
  • Siri / App Intents — to let you save a spot by voice

Optional external links

Some features let you open a saved location in Google Maps, Waze, or Google Street View. These only launch when you explicitly tap them, and only send the coordinates needed to open that location — nothing else about your Vantage data is shared. Use of those apps/services is governed by their own privacy policies.

AI-assisted import (optional, manual)

Vantage can import locations from a JSON file or pasted text that you produce yourself using an AI chat tool of your choice. Vantage does not connect to any AI service directly — you copy a prompt from Vantage, use it in whatever AI tool you choose, and paste the result back in. Any data shared with that AI tool is between you and that tool's own provider.

Data deletion

Deleting an entry in Vantage deletes it from your iCloud private database. Deleting the app removes all local data; your iCloud copy can be removed by deleting the entries first, or via iCloud storage management in iOS/macOS Settings.
